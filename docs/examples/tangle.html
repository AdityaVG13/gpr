Tangle-style scrubbable metrics

Drag any underlined number in the prose to scrub its value. Every dependent figure on the page recomputes live. Bret Victor's Tangle pattern, here applied to budget and intent count for a what-if-we-doubled-this exploration.

MechanicEach scrubbable span declares data-scrub="key" with min/max/step. Pointer-drag changes the value; a small reactive engine recomputes derived values declared on other spans (data-formula="key * rate"). ~80 LOC vanilla.

Try dragging the underlined numbers

The current Plan declares 5 intents averaging 6 iterations each, at 80k tokens per iteration. The build agent runs at $15 per million tokens.

That works out to 30 iterations, 2,400,000 tokens, and roughly $36.00 in build cost — before any Layer-2 audits run.

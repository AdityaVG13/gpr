concept · 04

Matuschak-style stacked columns

Cmd-click any link below to push a new column beside the current one instead of navigating away. Build a horizontal stack of side-by-side reading panes; scroll horizontally to walk back through them. Use it to compare two intents, two revisions, or two acceptance criteria without losing your place.

MechanicClick handler intercepts cmd-click (or shift-click); resolves the linked section's HTML, clones it into a new column appended to the stack flex container, then scrolls horizontally to it. Each pushed column gets a close button.
